## Check 3 — Do names read naturally for a shopper?

**PASS, with 2 MINOR-MAJOR findings and a pattern of cosmetic truncation.**

Full-set scan of all 91 published names (not just the 37-offer sample) for the specific defect classes named in the brief:

- **Run-together words (the original WP-10 defect):** **0 found.** Programmatic scan for "no space + long token" hits only `Schweinshalssteaks`, which is one legitimate German compound word (pork-neck steaks), not a fused multi-word string. Matches the funnel's own `possiblyFusedName: 0`.
- **Umlaut correctness:** every umlaut/accent checked by eye reads correctly — `Wyländer Rauchmöckli`, `Züribieter Joghurt`, `Sélection Trauben`, `Le Gruyère`, `Spécialité Suisse`, `Äpfel Gala`, `Gesamtes Ceylor-, Cosano- und Feelgood-Sortiment`. No stripped umlauts found (matches the RCA's "umlauts 0 → 18" claim generalising further on this live run).
- **Badge text as the product name (BIOSUISSE/VEGAN/etc. standing in for a name):** **0 found.** Programmatic scan of all 91 names for all-caps standalone tokens and for BIOSUISSE/VEGAN/IP-SUISSE/CUMULUS found only one hit, `Bratspeck, IP-SUISSE` (see below) — never a badge alone as the entire name.
- **", IP-SUISSE" trailing-label pattern:** 1 instance, `Bratspeck, IP-SUISSE`. This is the re-review's own documented, accepted deviation (d) — the real printed line is "Bratspeck, IP-SUISSE" and narrowing the badge match to a standalone line was required to fix the p6 cross-column pairing. Truthful, not misleading, and already flagged as a non-blocking follow-up (NIT-R2) in the review. **Not a new defect.**

**New pattern found (not named in the RCA/review as closed): continuation-line truncation on multi-line printed names.** Programmatic scan of all 91 names for trailing comma/dash/ampersand/conjunction found **6 names cut off mid-phrase**:

| Name as published | Printed in full as | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| `Migros Bio` (p6, 3.20/4.20) | "Migros Bio Wienerli" | **Moderate** — the published name gives no product information at all; a shopper cannot tell this is Wienerli (sausages) from "Migros Bio" alone, and the flyer has other "Migros Bio ___" products this could be confused with |
| `Spécialité Suisse` (p6, 6.95/8.80) | "Spécialité Suisse Bündner Rohschinken, IP-SUISSE" | **Moderate** — drops the only noun that says what the product is (dried beef) |
| `Reibkäse Emmentaler &` (p9, 6.95/8.85) | "Reibkäse Emmentaler & Le Gruyère, AOP" | Minor — ends on a dangling "&", looks visibly broken, but "Emmentaler" alone still identifies the cheese family |
| `Gesamte Ponti- und` (p11, 3.36/4.80) | "Gesamte Ponti- und Giacobazzi-Sortiment" | Minor — ends on the conjunction "und", reads as an unfinished sentence, though the price/pairing itself is correct (this was the fixed Gran Pavesi mispairing) |
| `Rindshuftsteaks,` / `Rindssiedfleisch mager,` / `Valflora Vollmilch UHT,` / `Alle Nature Clean-Reinigungsmittel,` | full printed name continues after the comma (badge or a second product-line qualifier) | Cosmetic only — trailing comma, product still identifiable |
| `Migros Kalbs geschnetzeltes` (p5, 3.50/3.95) | printed as "Migros Kalbs-" / "geschnetzeltes" across a line break | Minor — the line-break hyphen was replaced with a space rather than removed, so a single German compound word ("Kalbsgeschnetzeltes") now reads as two disconnected words. Comprehensible, but not how the phrase is meant to read |

**Assessment:** none of these are the WP-10 defect class (run-together words, umlaut stripping, wrong product/price pairing) and none change what a shopper is charged. Two (`Migros Bio`, `Spécialité Suisse`) are severe enough as *naming* defects that a shopper could not identify the product from the name shown on basketch alone — worth a follow-up ticket on the continuation-line join logic (`joinNameParts`), separate from WP-10's scope. Not a blocker for this work package, which was scoped to OCR engine + price/name geometric pairing, not to multi-line name completeness — but flagging per the QA agent's mandate to report everything found.
